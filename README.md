# AI Voice Shield Pro

**AI Voice Shield Pro** is an advanced cybersecurity and real-time audio analysis system designed to detect and prevent AI-generated voice cloning, deepfake audio, and vishing (voice phishing) attacks. It helps individuals and organizations verify the authenticity of voice notes, live calls, and audio files instantly.

## 🚀 Key Features
* **Real-Time Audio Analysis:** Instantly processes streaming audio or voice clips with ultra-low latency.
* **Multi-Indicator Detection:** Evaluates tone, breathing patterns, frequency spectrums, and response timing.
* **Smart Alerts & Risk Scoring:** Delivers a clear authenticity score and actionable warnings before responding to suspicious audio.
* **Multi-Format Support:** Compatible with MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, and OGG formats.
